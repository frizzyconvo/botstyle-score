BB Botstyle Score

A rough automated read on how much a character card's description and greeting(s) lean on stock phrasings and words of AI models. It is not an AI detector, and it does not measure whether you used AI or how good your card is.

How it works

What it measures

Words, phrases and sentence habits that AI models use far more than human fiction writers ("a testament to", "the air was thick with", "a flicker of…", not-x-but-y). Each word and phrase is weighted by how much more often AI fiction uses it than human fiction. Only prose is scored: W++/PList blocks, key-value lists and pasted instructions are set aside.

The score

60% slop words, 25% slop phrases, 15% sentence structures, plus up to 6 points for heavy em-dash use and 25 per obvious chatbot leftover ("Certainly! Here is…", "I hope this helps!"). Each part is scaled so 100 means "as sloppy as the sloppiest tenth of card-length AI fiction", so the score reads as a share of peak slop. The ⓘ next to a result says where it sits among real human and AI-written cards.

Bands: Original under 20, Mixed 20–40, Common 40+. About four in five human cards from 2023 land in Original; most AI-written cards land in Mixed or Common.

Not an AI detector

It measures a house style, not authorship. A human who writes in that style scores high (pulp fantasy and romance share a lot of it), and a model told to avoid it can score low. It also says nothing about whether a card is good.

The leaderboard

Models: character cards each model wrote for us from the same requests: the name, creator note and tags of real 2023 human cards, asked for in seven formats (W++/PList, lists, sections, plain prose, first person, interview, and a lazy "make me a bot"). Only those plain requests are ranked; the report for each model also shows cards written with "don't sound like AI" instructions and rewrites of human cards. Bars show the middle half of each model's cards, the line marks the median. Small samples are marked.

References: real card sets scored with the same version: early 2023 human cards (Korshuk, JanitorAI, Chub), later Chub and Perchance cards (a mix of human and AI-assisted writing), and AI-written persona sets.

Texture (experimental)

AI text tends to keep paragraphs close to the same length. The report shows that evenness and a likelihood combining it with the score. It isn't part of the score: careful human writers can be even too.

Your cards and privacy

Cards are scored in your browser. "Your cards" is stored in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you press "Share to public list" (when that list is switched on), which sends the card's name, link, score, version and what you tick, never the card text.

Versions

Every score carries the scorer version, so results can be compared after the word lists or calibration change. Details, data and code: BOTSTYLE.md in the repository.
