How it works

What this is

Botstyle Score measures how much a character card's writing leans on the stock phrasing AI models fall back on. It's a fork of slop-score by Sam Paech, rebuilt for character cards.

Not an AI detector

It measures AI house style, not authorship. A human who writes in that style scores high, and a model told to avoid it can score low. Read the score as "how much does this sound like default AI writing", not "was this written by AI".

Botstyle Score (this fork)

Built for character cards (description + greeting). Only the prose is scored: W++/PList blocks, key-value lists and copy-pasted instructions are tallied and set aside, and the score comes with a confidence range that widens when there isn't much prose.

  • 60% - Slop words: words models use far more than human fiction writers, each weighted by how lopsided that is
  • 25% - Slop phrases: two- and three-word phrases, weighted the same way
  • 15% - Sentence structures: not-x-but-y plus AI sentence habits ("a hint of…", "the air was thick…", "like a promise", "…," she purred)
  • + up to 6 for heavy em-dash use (over 6 per 1,000 words), +25 each for obvious AI tells (a chatbot's "Certainly! Here is…" or "I hope this helps!")

100 on a component means "as sloppy as the sloppiest 10% of card-length AI fiction". Each card is also compared with 2023 cards of the same style (free prose, headers + prose, W++/lists, in-voice…). Shared clichés, human tells, craft notes and AI-favourite names (Elara, Kael, Thorne…) are shown but not scored.

Writing style: Original (under 20), Mixed (20–40), Common (40 and up). About 87% of human cards from early 2023 come out Original; AI-written cards mostly land in Mixed or Common. Description format (free prose, formatted prose, compact W++/PList/JSON, lists, in-voice, hybrids) describes the layout and isn't scored. Bonus tags (Copyedit-proof, Clichemonger, A card for ants, …) flag cards in roughly the top few percent of real cards for that trait. Details: BOTSTYLE.md in the repository.

How to read the score

The 0–100 number isn't a percentage of anything, so each result also says where it sits: higher than N% of human-written cards from 2023 and M% of AI-written cards. A score above most human cards but below most AI cards is in between, and the Mixed band says the same thing.

Texture (experimental)

AI text tends to keep paragraphs close to the same length. The texture line reports that evenness and a likelihood that combines it with the score. It isn't part of the score yet: careful human writers can be even too.

Stats for nerds

The collapsed section keeps the original slop-score metrics (its slop score, slop word and trigram rates, Flesch-Kincaid level, sentence and paragraph length, dialogue frequency, MATTR). On character cards they barely tell human and AI writing apart, so they're there for curiosity, not judgement.

Leaderboard

Models are scored on their creative-writing outputs from a standard set of prompts, cut to card length. Lower scores mean less stock AI phrasing. These outputs were also used to build the word lists, so model scores are in-sample.
